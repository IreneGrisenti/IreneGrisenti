Welcome to my collection, please handle with humor. 

## Current status and provenance

I'm a data science student with a background in digital humanities, which means I spent a lot of time in libraries and archives.  
The two have more in common than you'd think: both are about finding useful information in mountains of materials. 

I'm living in Sweden, but I'm originally from Italy, where I spent 25 years taking sunlight for granted.

I'm currently doing my **data science internship at StickerApp AB**, where the focus is on business customers.  
My job is to catalogue them properly: modeling the data, cleaning it up and enriching it with information from other sources.

The final purpose of the collection is still being appraised, but the candidates are:
- predicting customer behavior
- giving sales a tool to decide who to reach out to
- identifying new customers who look like the best ones we already have
